Make It Rain Before Curfew

Official Rules for the Confessions Afta Curfew High Score Contest

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. This is a game of skill. The highest verified score wins.

1. Who Runs It

This contest is run by Confessions Afta Curfew, a podcast created by Buhzz Taylor in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (the "Sponsor"). Instagram, Meta, and YouTube do not sponsor, endorse, or run this contest.

2. Who Can Play

You must be 18 or older and a legal resident of the United States to win. Confessions Afta Curfew hosts, guests, crew, and their households are not eligible to win. Void where prohibited.

3. Contest Dates

The contest starts December 1, 2026 at 12:00 AM Eastern and ends December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern. Only games played and submitted during that window count. Games played before December 1 are practice rounds and do not show a verification code.

4. How To Play

  1. Tap Get Your Free Code in the game and join the free Confessions Afta Curfew email list. Your access code is emailed to you. You can unsubscribe any time. Texts are optional.
  2. Go to buhzz.com/game.html, enter your Instagram username and the access code, and play. Without a code you can play up to 3 practice rounds a day, but practice scores do not count. With a code you can play as much as you want.
  3. When your game ends, take a screenshot of your score card. It shows your score, your username, and a verification code.

5. How To Submit A Score

Send your screenshot to ConfessionsAftaCurfew@gmail.com or by DM to @confessionsaftacurfew on Instagram. Include your Instagram username, phone number, and email address. Submissions must be received by December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern.

You can play and submit as many times as you want. Only your highest verified score counts.

6. Verifying Scores

  • Every score is checked against its verification code. A score whose code does not match, or that looks edited in any way, is disqualified.
  • The Sponsor may ask any player to prove their score is real at any time before the winner is announced, including by sending a new video of a full game or playing live while the Sponsor watches. A player who is asked and does not do it can be disqualified.
  • Cheating, editing screenshots, hacking the game, or using bots or scripts gets you disqualified from the contest.

7. Ties

If two or more players tie for the highest score, they face off head to head. Each player gets one attempt to play the game while the Sponsor watches live. The higher score wins the prize. If they tie again, they repeat the face off until there is a winner.

8. The Prize

One winner receives one official Confessions Afta Curfew bookbag, approximate retail value $50. Shipping within the United States is free. The prize cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash. If the bookbag becomes unavailable, the Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value.

9. Winner Announcement

The winner will be announced in January 2027 on Confessions Afta Curfew and on Instagram at @confessionsaftacurfew. The winner will be contacted using the info they submitted and must respond within 7 days with a shipping address. If the winner does not respond, the next highest verified score wins.

10. Your Info

Your username, phone number, and email are used to verify scores, contact the winner, and, if you joined the list, to send you Confessions Afta Curfew emails. Your username and score may be shown on the leaderboard and in weekly contest posts. We never sell your info.

11. The Fine Print

By entering, you agree to these rules and to the Sponsor's decisions, which are final. The Sponsor may change, pause, or cancel the contest if something outside its control, such as a technical problem or fraud, affects how the contest runs. The winner is responsible for any taxes on the prize.

Questions? Email ConfessionsAftaCurfew@gmail.com.

Kings and Queens, Peace.
